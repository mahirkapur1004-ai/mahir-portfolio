About me

senior 3D artist · 3D generalist · gurugram, india

I'm Mahir Kapur, a Senior 3D Artist and 3D Generalist with 5+ years of hands-on experience delivering photorealistic real-time and path-traced work in Unreal Engine 5, Blender and Substance Painter, including AR/VR asset work for Amazon's immersive commerce and virtual try-on initiatives.

My day-to-day is automotive: taking raw manufacturer CAD, retopologising it into clean quad meshes, hand-building PBR paint, chrome and glass, and lighting it with Lumen and path tracing, so that one asset can run as a browser configurator, a VR demo and a launch film. I've led four to five junior artists through texturing and retopology, and I like raising visual quality and production speed with new and AI-assisted workflows.
